01 / GET TO KNOW US

One foundation.
Many ways to help.

InAmigos Foundation was founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO). Based in Chhattisgarh, the Section 8 non-profit works with professionals, volunteers and corporate partners to create social impact across India.

03 / OUR CAUSES

Six causes.
One purpose.

Different problems need different kinds of action. The initiatives below cover immediate support, opportunity, compassion and long-term sustainability.

01

PROJECT SEVA

Food & clothing

Providing food and clothing to underprivileged communities and helping address essential needs with dignity.

02

PROJECT BACHPANSHAALA

Quality education

Supporting quality education for underprivileged children and creating stronger learning opportunities.

03

PROJECT JEEV

Animal welfare

Supporting stray and abandoned animals through feeding, protection and welfare initiatives.

04

PROJECT UDAAN

Women empowerment

Promoting skill development and financial independence so women can build stronger, more self-reliant futures.

05

PROJECT PRAKRITI

Planet & sustainability

Environmental conservation and sustainability efforts, including plantation drives and awareness campaigns.

06

PROJECT VIKAS

Skills & employability

Bridging education and employability through internships, skill development, career guidance and practical exposure.
